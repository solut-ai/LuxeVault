Bond clock · CBB3 · Interactive

Bond clock — the record kept with the Treasury

The same bond clock presented on the OTFZ Treasury page, kept here as a record. It keeps itself from the system clock: for every date in the verified daily table — Oct 25, 2025 through Jan 18, 2027 — it reads the value straight from that table row, exactly as attested; past Jan 18, 2027 it extends the same record with the cycle formula, each 90-day cycle base rolled up × 1.025 at rollover and accruing linearly day by day. Pick any date — or step day by day — and every figure on this page, including bond depth, Total Ecosystem net value, and the VERITAS bond-clock line, recalculates from that date. Nothing is updated by hand.
